# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Examples of positive behavior include:

- being considerate and constructive;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting actionable feedback;
- focusing discussion on the project and its users;
- protecting other people's privacy and confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sharing
credentials, private prompts, proprietary source code, and unredacted agent
sessions without authorization.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through the hosting
platform's private contact or moderation tools. Do not include sensitive sample
data in a public report.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as
practical.
